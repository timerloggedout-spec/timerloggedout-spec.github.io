# Project glossary

> Definitions for recurring terms used across the Autonomous AI Employees site and its linked engineering projects. These descriptions are orientation aids; consult the linked source specifications for normative definitions.

## Agent fleet

A group of AI collaborators coordinated under human direction to perform research, implementation, review, and operational tasks.

## Evidence receipt

A durable record that identifies the source, observation time, result, and provenance of a claim or operation. A receipt is stronger than an unsupported narrative assertion.

## Tribute

The public evidence and control surface for the upstream contributor lane. It projects repository evidence and is not itself the canonical source of implementation state.

## Lane matrix

A structured view of workstreams, their roles, boundaries, and coordination relationships.

## Termux monorepo

The primary repository used to coordinate development, automation, architecture documentation, and evidence workflows for this project.

## StudySprint

A study-planning project built around an evidence-gated foundation.

## Delphi Oracle

An evidence-gate oracle project that evaluates supplied or fetched evidence and records a decision with provenance.

## Repo+Story

A repository-history narrative concept that groups commits and file mutations into cited change stories with impact context.

## SHE

The project’s System Health and Evidence dashboard architecture. Consult the termux-monorepo documentation for its current contracts and implementation status.

## ATES

Agent Throughput and Effectiveness Score: a project metric family for observing agent work. It should be interpreted alongside quality and evidence, not as a substitute for correctness.

## 3L0 / Moneyball

Project terminology for evidence-based evaluation and comparison of agents, orchestration policies, and outcomes. Consult the source documentation for current definitions and formulas.

## Proposed, implemented, executed, validated, promoted

Distinct lifecycle states. A proposal is not an implementation; a commit is not runtime execution; a successful check is not necessarily production promotion.
