Terminology and definitions

Glossary

Shared vocabulary for reading the public project pages, operational dashboards, and evidence records.

Evidence and provenance

Evidence
A source-backed observation that can be inspected or independently retrieved. A summary or assertion without a supporting source is not equivalent to evidence.
Receipt
A structured record of a decision or operation, including the inputs, outcome, version, and provenance needed to audit what happened.
Provenance
The origin and observation context of a record: where it came from, which artifact or API path supplied it, and when it was observed.
Freshness
Whether an observation is recent enough for the decision being made. A timestamp or expiry bound makes freshness explicit; old data is not silently treated as current.
Asserted evidence
A statement supplied by a person or agent but not independently verified against its source. It may guide retrieval, but it is not proof by itself.
Content hash
A cryptographic digest used to identify exact content bytes and detect changes. A hash supports integrity checking; it does not establish that the source claim is true.

Execution and delivery states

COMMITTED
A change exists in version-control history. This alone does not prove that automation executed or that the change is deployed.
EXECUTED
The relevant job or process actually ran, with runtime evidence such as job and step results.
VALIDATED
The expected outputs and quality requirements were checked against observed evidence.
PROMOTED
The change was deliberately moved into its target production or canonical state. Promotion is distinct from commit, execution, and validation.
PARTIAL
Only a bounded portion of the intended dataset or operation is complete. Partial data must not be described as complete.
STALE
The displayed observation is older than its freshness policy or the latest known source state.

Agent orchestration and measurement

Agent
An automated software actor that performs a bounded task using tools, instructions, and an execution environment.
Manager / orchestrator
The policy layer that assigns work, selects agents, coordinates dependencies, manages retries, and decides when to integrate or escalate.
ATES
Agent Throughput and Effectiveness Score: a family of throughput and execution-efficiency observations. It is not a substitute for correctness or quality evidence.
WTCV
Weighted Task Completion Value: a throughput/effectiveness measure whose interpretation depends on task weight and outcome quality.
3L0 / MoneyBall
The project's evidence-oriented evaluation approach for comparing agent and manager outcomes using quality, integration, time, context use, retries, conflicts, and human intervention rather than raw activity alone.
Lane Matrix
A generated projection that groups pull requests into operational lanes for triage. It is a navigation and prioritization aid, not merge authorization.
