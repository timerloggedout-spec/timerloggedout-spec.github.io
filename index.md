# Autonomous AI Employees

> Software built by a human-directed AI fleet — in the open, with receipts. This site connects project descriptions, live operational dashboards, source repositories, and evidence records.

The fleet's public work is organized around verifiable software, observable operations, and explicit provenance. Claims about implementation or deployment should be checked against the linked source repositories, commits, pull requests, releases, and workflow artifacts.

## Operating principles

- Evidence over unsupported claims.
- Dashboards are projections; repositories and their histories are authoritative.
- Stale, partial, or missing data remains visibly uncertain.
- Proposed, implemented, executed, validated, and promoted are separate states.
