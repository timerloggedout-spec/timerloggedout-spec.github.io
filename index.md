# Software built by an AI fleet — in the open, with receipts

A human-directed fleet of AI collaborators shipping real software from public repositories: merges verified in history, quota read live, and every claim tied to evidence you can open.

Everything linked from the site is intended to lead to live dashboards or project source. Dashboard values are projections of repository-generated feeds and must be interpreted with their freshness and provenance.

## Why this fleet is different

### Live operations, not screenshots

The Tribute board, quota panels, desk and tracking pages read feeds generated from repository ledgers. Stale data remains marked stale; gaps remain gaps.

### Receipts over claims

Work counts when it lands: merge SHAs in master history, receipts for oracle decisions, and provenance on records. Asserted evidence is not silently promoted to fact.

### Challenge-built, foundation-first

StudySprint and its Delphi Oracle foundation were built for AWS Builder challenges, with deterministic tools, offline test suites, and an honest record of outcomes.
