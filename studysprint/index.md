# StudySprint — a study plan you can trust

> A verified syllabus becomes a week-by-week study plan, recall quizzes, and re-plans on missed weeks. Trust receipt before planning; missed sessions are re-planned without guilt.

StudySprint is a Strands-wired agent built for first-generation college students. It is designed to turn a syllabus into a useful daily study plan while making the evidence behind that plan visible.

## Trust receipt before planning

Before planning, StudySprint presents what it fetched: course, weeks, due dates, and an evidence hash. Planning proceeds only after the student confirms the receipt. If a syllabus claim cannot be verified, the agent abstains instead of planning from unsupported information.

The published page includes a sample synthetic BIO 101 syllabus and a sample receipt. Sample values are illustrative and should not be mistaken for a user's actual course data.

## Product capabilities

- **Today view:** today's sessions, with a direct start action and a missed-session action.
- **Time-budget reflow:** re-plan around the time a student actually has available.
- **Daily check-in:** done, partial, or missed per session.
- **Recovered weeks:** missed work is carried forward; the design avoids broken-streak guilt mechanics.
- **Plain-language mode:** agent actions are explained in accessible language.
- **Week-by-week plan and quizzes:** learn and retrieve blocks with recall quizzes.
- **Multi-course and calendar support:** the page describes multi-course planning and ICS export.
- **Reading pipeline:** a multi-pass reader for syllabus/course materials.

## How it works

1. Bring a syllabus.
2. Inspect and confirm the evidence receipt.
3. Study from today's plan.
4. Re-plan freely when time or circumstances change.
