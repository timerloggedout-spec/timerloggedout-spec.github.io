# Termux Monorepo — project hub

> Primary development and management repository for the timerloggedout-spec engineering fleet.

The termux-monorepo brings together project architecture, automation, CI/CD, agent orchestration, operational dashboards, research lanes, and evidence workflows. ## Evidence discipline

Do not treat a planned feature as implemented, a commit as proof of execution, a queued workflow as success, or a dashboard as the canonical record. Follow each claim to its repository evidence and preserve unknown or partial states.
